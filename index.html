About me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. So far my projects include a subthreshold bandgap voltage reference in SKY130, a four-layer ARINC-429 interface board, and an STM32 control platform. I like the debugging as much as the design: reading datasheets closely, checking the supply rails before powering a board, and working out why a simulation doesn't match the hand calculation.

Analog IC design

MOSFET-Only Subthreshold Bandgap Voltage Reference

A SKY130 bandgap reference built from subthreshold MOSFETs, with PTAT/CTAT compensation, an error amplifier with Miller compensation, and full temperature and PSRR characterization: about 89 ppm/°C and 65 dB low-frequency PSRR in simulation.
